Try this

  1. Click Overfit, then drag the epoch timeline from the left. The boundary goes from a straight-ish line (underfit), to following the wave (good), to wrapping around ringed noisy points (overfit).
  2. Still on Overfit, move L2 to 0.003. The islands around noisy points disappear and the gap shrinks.
  3. Now push L2 to 0.03. The gap is tiny, but test accuracy falls too. That's underfitting from too much regularization.
  4. Back on Overfit, compare 30 training points with 300. More data makes memorizing harder.
  5. Set Label noise to 0%. With nothing wrong to memorize, even the big model does fine.
  6. Click New data sample a few times on Overfit, then on Good fit. The overfit boundary changes wildly with each sample; the good one barely moves.
