Pit-wall debrief

Ask the class each question before revealing the answer. By the time the terms land, they already own the mental model.

Q1 Which team finishes a single lap faster? Watch one lap on each track. ▸
What students sayTeam Sprinter — they don't stop.
The ML revealA lap is an epoch. Team Sprinter is Batch Gradient Descent (BGD). Because it processes all the data at once and updates only once, a single epoch runs incredibly fast.
Q2 After one lap, whose car actually handles better? Count the adjustments each team made. ▸
What students sayTeam Tweaker's car — they adjusted it 20 times, not just once.
The ML revealEvery adjustment is a weight update. Team Tweaker is Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD). By updating the weights continuously through the lap, the model learns far more per epoch.
Q3 To win you need 100% setup quality. Who gets there first? Ignore lap time — watch the quality bars and the clock. ▸
What students sayTeam Tweaker! Each lap is slow (~8s), but they hit 100% in just 3 laps. Sprinter's laps are quick (~2s) but they need 50 of them.
The ML revealThis is convergence. SGD converges in far fewer epochs. Each epoch takes longer on the clock, but the total number of epochs needed to reach minimum loss drops dramatically.
Numbers are teaching values · Sprinter: 2.0s/lap, +1 update · Tweaker: 8.0s/lap, +20 updates
