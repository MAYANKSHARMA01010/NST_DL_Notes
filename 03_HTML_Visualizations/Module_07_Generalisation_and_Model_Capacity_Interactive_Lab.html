pocket reference

Four ideas to keep

Underfitting
High train error + high validation error. Often high bias.
Overfitting
Very low train error + much higher validation error. Often high variance.
Variance
Requires imagining or performing retraining on different sampled datasets.
Double descent
A possible pattern—not a promise that increasing capacity always helps.
